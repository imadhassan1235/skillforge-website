PROGRAM STRUCTURE

A structured learning journey.

Selected interns will progress through a practical workflow designed to help them learn, build and improve their professional portfolio.

01

Orientation

Understand the internship structure, selected department, communication channels, tools, expectations and workflow.

02

Practical Tasks

Complete progressive assignments designed around your selected internship track and receive guidance where needed.

03

Project Work

Apply your skills to individual or team-based projects and maintain your work in a professional GitHub or portfolio workflow where applicable.

04

Portfolio & Career Development

Improve your GitHub, portfolio, CV and professional presence while learning how to present your work.

05

Final Evaluation

Complete the required internship work and receive performance feedback based on the program requirements.

06

Completion & Certificate

Interns who successfully complete the applicable internship requirements may receive a SkillForge internship completion certificate.
